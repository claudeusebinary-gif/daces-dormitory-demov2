Terms of Service

Terms of stay · Last updated November 2, 2026

1. Acceptance of these terms

By creating an account, booking a bed, or staying at D'aces Dormitory, you agree to these Terms of Service and to our Privacy Policy and House Rules. If you do not agree, please do not use the website or book a bed.

2. Your account

  • You must give true and complete information, including your name, contact details, and a valid government-issued ID.
  • You are responsible for keeping your password private and for everything done through your account.
  • Guests may browse the home page, dorm layout, rooms, and location, but only signed-in tenants can book or manage a stay.

3. Booking and admin approval

  • When you pick a bed, we hold it for you for 15 minutes while you complete the booking form.
  • A submitted booking is not final. The dormitory admin must review and approve it first. Until then your booking shows as "waiting for admin approval" and the room does not appear in your tenant details.
  • We may decline a booking, for example if the bed is no longer available or the information is incomplete. If declined, the bed is released and you may book again.

4. Rent and fees

  • Rent is charged monthly per bed at the rate shown for the room. Your next rent payment is due 30 days after your move-in date.
  • The reservation fee is non-refundable. The security fee and clean fee are shown in your booking summary before you pay.
  • Late or unpaid rent may lead to reminders, restricted access to some services, and, if it continues, ending of the stay.

5. Length of stay, transfers, and move-out

  • You choose an expected length of stay when you book (for example 1 month, 3 months, 6 months, or 1 year).
  • Room or bed transfers are subject to availability and admin approval.
  • To leave, submit a move-out request from your Settings page and settle any unpaid balance before your move-out date.

6. Using the room and shared areas

Each bed is for one tenant only. You may not sublet your bed, let others stay overnight without approval, or use the room for any business. Please follow the House Rules at all times.

7. Damages and lost items

You are responsible for damage you or your visitors cause to the room, furniture, or shared facilities, and the cost of repair may be charged to you. The dormitory is not responsible for lost, stolen, or damaged personal belongings. Keep valuables locked.

8. Ending a stay

We may end a stay with written notice for serious or repeated violations of these terms or the House Rules, non-payment, or conduct that puts other tenants at risk. You may end your stay by following the move-out steps above.

9. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms from time to time. The "last updated" date above shows the latest version. Continuing to use the website or stay after a change means you accept it.

10. Contact us

Questions about these terms? Use the Message admin button in your account, or the contact details in the website footer.

Last updated November 2, 2026

D'aces Dormitory respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it, in line with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: full name, email address, and password (stored in a scrambled, hashed form).
  • Booking and tenant details: date of birth, sex, civil status, occupation, school or employer, mobile number, and home address.
  • Emergency contact: the name, relationship, phone number, and address of the person we may contact for you.
  • Identity document: your ID type, ID number, and a photo of your valid ID.
  • Stay and payment records: your room and bed, move-in date, rent, payments made, and billing email.
  • Messages and requests: chats, complaints, suggestions, and maintenance requests you send us.

2. Why we collect it

  • To create your account and process, approve, and manage your booking.
  • To verify your identity and keep the dormitory safe.
  • To collect rent, record payments, and send receipts and reminders.
  • To respond to your messages, complaints, and repair requests.
  • To contact your emergency contact if something happens to you.

3. Who can see your information

Only the dormitory admin and authorized staff can see your tenant records. Other tenants only see what you choose to share, such as your name and room in the marketplace and chat. We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with payment providers needed to process your payments, or when the law requires it.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your information while you are a tenant and for a reasonable period afterwards for billing, legal, and security purposes, then delete or anonymize it.

5. How we protect it

  • Passwords are hashed, never stored as plain text.
  • Access to tenant records is limited to authorized admins.
  • Our forms are protected against common web attacks, and sessions end when you log out.

6. Cookies

We use only the cookies needed to keep you signed in and to protect your forms. We do not use advertising cookies.

7. Your rights

You have the right to be informed, to access and correct your information, to object to or withdraw your consent, and to ask us to delete your information where the law allows. You can update most details in your Profile and Settings pages, or message the admin for anything else.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do. For privacy questions, use Message admin in your account or the contact details in the footer.

Quick answers about staying at D'aces Dormitory

How do I book a bed?

Create an account and log in, open Rooms, pick a room, choose an available bed and deck (lower or upper), and click Book this room. Fill in your details, choose a payment method, and confirm.

Why does my booking say "waiting for admin approval"?

Every new booking is reviewed by the dormitory admin first. Your bed is held for you in the meantime, and the room appears in your details once the admin approves it. You will get a notification either way.

What do I need to book?

Your personal and contact details, an emergency contact, one valid government-issued ID (JPG or PNG, up to 5MB), and your preferred move-in date.

What payment methods can I use?

GCash is available now. Debit/credit card and bank transfer are not available yet.

When is my rent due?

Your next rent payment is due 30 days after your move-in date. You can see what you owe and your payment history on the Payments page.

Is the reservation fee refundable?

No. The reservation fee is non-refundable, as stated before you pay.

What is included in the room?

Free Wi-Fi, a study area, 24/7 water and electricity, CCTV security, and a gate and fence. Rooms have shared cabinets, their own toilet and bath, a kitchen for everyone, and a free electric fan.

Something in my room is broken. What do I do?

Go to your Profile and click Request Maintenance, choose a category and urgency, and describe the problem. The dorm team will follow up.

Can I change rooms or leave early?

Yes. Use the room transfer or move-out request in your Settings page. Transfers depend on availability and admin approval.

How do I contact the admin?

Use the Ask Admin button on the Dorm page or Message admin on the Rooms page to chat with the dormitory admin directly, or use the contact details in the footer.

Can I look around without an account?

Yes. Choose Continue as Guest to view the home page, dorm layout, rooms, and location. You need an account to book or use other features.

Please read and follow these rules so everyone can live comfortably

Respect and quiet

  1. Be respectful to your roommates, other tenants, staff, and neighbors.
  2. Quiet hours are from 10:00 PM to 6:00 AM. Keep music, calls, and gatherings low during this time.
  3. Settle disagreements calmly. If you cannot, ask the admin to help.

Visitors and security

  1. Visitors are allowed only in common areas from 8:00 AM to 9:00 PM and must be accompanied by the tenant.
  2. No overnight guests without the admin's approval.
  3. The gate closes at 11:00 PM. Tell the admin ahead of time if you will come home late.
  4. Never prop the gate or doors open, and do not share your key or access with anyone.
  5. Report lost keys, suspicious people, or anything unsafe to the admin right away.

Cleanliness

  1. Keep your bed, cabinet, and area clean and tidy.
  2. Wash your dishes and clean the kitchen after you use it. Do not leave food out.
  3. Segregate your trash and throw it in the proper bins.
  4. Leave the shared toilet and bath clean for the next person.

Safety and use of the room

  1. No smoking or vaping inside the building.
  2. No illegal drugs, weapons, or open flames. No cooking inside the sleeping rooms.
  3. Do not bring heaters, extra air conditioners, or other high-power appliances without approval.
  4. Turn off lights, fans, and appliances when you leave the room.
  5. No pets unless the admin has approved it.
  6. Do not move, remove, or damage furniture and fixtures. Report repairs through Request Maintenance.

Payments

  1. Pay your rent on or before the due date.
  2. Tell the admin early if you will have trouble paying.

If a rule is broken

A first violation usually gets a verbal reminder, then a written warning. Serious or repeated violations may lead to fees for damages or the ending of the stay, as explained in the Terms of Service.
